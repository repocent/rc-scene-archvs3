KU Crystals (White)

November 23, 2023

A fake KU vendor recently cheated me out of 50 USD. He seemed to be one of those KU Crystal vendors who are plenty around, but, unfortunately, a bunch of scammers have mingled with the game recently.

In the scammer’s defense: He told me he could not provide me with samples. I thought that was some language barrier issue because he still took my money. Next time, I will know better. Another misunderstanding: He still wanted to book an ad on RC SCENE. He thought I would accept any ad. Another language barrier classic in the shady drug forums circus.

My second approach was more successful. I received 5 grams of the White KU Crystal product, making this blog post 100 USD in production costs.

What is KU?

Until summer, KU Crystals used to be brownish (maybe some Eutylone analogue), but as far as I understand, the recipe needed to be changed for legal reasons. The current product is said to be “N-cyclohexyl-N-methyl-Methylone, a replacement of Cyputylone,” according to a post by Jiu Jitsu in our Telegram group (https://t.me/rcscene/40377). It’s solid white crystals now, and by solid, I mean I slightly damaged my credit card when trying to split one into two.

To my surprise, the product was the same substance I had had two years ago when I ordered “Cocolino” from a Polish Vendor. KU Crystals from China are pure and not cut with sticky sugar like the variant Polish dilution experts offer, but they’re identical substances.

I do not consider having KU crystals stimulating effects. I find it too mild to attribute any psychoactive effects other than I did 5 grams, although I had ordered it to have it tested in a lab. There must be something about it. Not too much, though. I am not going to order KU again. There are more exciting compounds available.
