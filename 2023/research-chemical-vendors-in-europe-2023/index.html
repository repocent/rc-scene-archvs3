European Consumer Network Online Dispute Resolution

  • If a vendor accepts your money they must deliver within 30 days the latest. If they can’t deliver, they are legally obliged to issue a full refund.
  • It does not matter what you ordered, if a vendor takes your money they must deliver or refund you. You do not pay for shipping, you pay for receiving your order!
  • The European Consumer Network assists you with whatever issues with foul-play vendors who take your money but never deliver and even try to make you believe you had to accept that ordering online would involve any risk. There is no such thing as a risk involved in online ordering. Vendor make so much profit the must deliver or refund. They can afford it, don’t worry about them.
  • Also, report fraud to your credit card company (they can help you get your money back), to your bank and to your crypto (Bitcoin) exchange. Report the fraud to them, they can seize the criminals funds!
