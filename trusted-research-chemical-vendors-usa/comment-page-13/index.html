      Due to the new European Postal Act that took effect as of 1 January 2020, Research Chemicals may no longer be simply sent in a standard envelopes with a simple stamp. International regulations require vendors to make a strict distinction between sending chemicals (for which must be used “professional mail”) and simple letters not containing products (which may of course still be sent via regular mail).

      It has become illegal to send chemicals with regular mail, you best choose a courier service, otherwise either Dutch postal service or US customs might silently confiscate and destroy your order, even if the letter contained unscheduled substances only. Their legal basis is this new EU regulation.

      In case you wonder why your untracked mail never arrives: Post NL are actively enforcing this new EU regulation. They would literally search mail for known patterns and destroy letters without even opening them. They of course know from where and when vendors would ship and what their envelopes look like. So do customs and police. The scene is closely monitored, police and the drug monitoring agency place test orders all the time.

      A couple of years ago, Dutch customs would send you an email if you regularly ordered research chemicals to remind you to maintain a healthy lifestyle. I used to think this was spam or a joke until I once was questioned by police and the narcotics officer asked me if I wanted to have a look at the info sheet about maintaining a healthy lifestyle that Dutch customs had added to my order when they confiscated the letter and forwarded it to local police… Don’t know if they still do this ;-D
