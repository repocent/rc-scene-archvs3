Research Chemicals / New Psychoactive Substances explained. ++ Latest update: Behind the curtains: Have a look at labs manufacturing RCs.

February 13, 2020

Videos and Photos showing Research Chemical manufacturers

There sure are better looking labs as well, but just like with deciding whether to join police or mafia, some things tend to be rather shady and remain in the dark, rather than others…

Just one thing: There are both hidden underground labs as well as regular labs producing Research Chemicals. The products are usually being manufactured in China, and in case China has banned a substance, European labs would order drug precursor substances from China which they would only finalize.

No matter how stylish a vendor’s website looks like, even expensive shops like RareChems tend to order the cheapest shit you could even think off. EuSynth, The Real RC and Chemical Collective sometimes order from places you would not want to have a meal at. In addition, shops like chemical collective add up to 50% cutting agent, which is even cheaper than cheap Research Chemicals, don’t trust the lab tests, they sometimes provide you. On the way from China to Lizard Labs to Chemical Collective Joke to your doorstep, heavy use of cutting agents is involved, that is a sure bet.

We received reports of e.g. Chemical Collective Joke providing lab results indicating 97% purity, which after an Acetone wash turned out to be less than 50% pure…
